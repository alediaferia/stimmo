01

OMI band, not a model

Italian per-transaction sale data isn't public. The €/m² Compr min–max band — built from observed sales by the Agenzia delle Entrate — is the spine of every estimate.

02

One file of coefficients

Floor, lift, energy, outdoor, era, orientation, amenities — every multiplier lives in valuation/adjustments.py. No black box, nothing learned, nothing hidden.

03

Ask-shifted, not rogito

Verdicts compare the asking price to an ask-shifted band (rogito +6 %) — a normal-market premium from listing to closing. ±5 % around it is "fair".
