How it works

§ 02 · Three steps
  1. 1
    Drag the pill above to your browser's bookmarks bar.
    Show it with ⌘⇧B on Mac, Ctrl⇧B on Windows.
  2. 2
    Open any listing on immobiliare.it in Milano.
    Works on the public listing detail page (/annunci/…). Map / search pages aren't supported.
  3. 3
    Click stimmo in your bookmarks bar.
    The script reads the listing's __NEXT_DATA__ JSON inside your tab and opens stimmo with the form prefilled. Nothing leaves your browser until you submit.
